Our story

The taste is worth your last cent

LeeJoys Achaar began in 2022 in Witbank, with small batches of achaar made by hand for family and neighbours.

That is still how it is done. Each tub is prepared in small batches so the flavour stays honest, and the heat is your choice: mild, medium or hot.

The kitchen has since grown into LeeJoy Express, a little shop where you can also refill water, pick up eggs, and enjoy fresh popcorn and potato twisters.
